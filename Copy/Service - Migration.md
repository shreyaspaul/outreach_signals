## **Migration FAQs**

### **Will our SEO break?**

Handled correctly, no.  
 There’s often a short adjustment period while Google re-crawls the new structure — this is normal for every migration.  
 We migrate metadata, slugs, headers, schema, redirects, alt tags, indexing rules, and internal linking.  
 Because Webflow loads faster and removes clutter, teams usually see equal or better performance within weeks.

### **Do you migrate all blog posts and categories?**

Yes.  
 Posts, categories, tags, media, authors, dates, redirects — everything.  
 We can also help clean outdated or low-performing content.

### **Can you match our current design exactly?**

Yes, if that’s what you want.  
 But most WordPress designs feel theme-restricted.  
 If your product has evolved, a refresh is usually the better option.

### **What if our WordPress backend is extremely messy?**

If it’s workable, we migrate it.  
 If it’s structurally broken (nested builders, unnamed classes, conflicting plugins), rebuilding is cheaper, faster, and more reliable.  
 We always give you the honest recommendation upfront.

### **Will our animations carry over?**

We rebuild them using Webflow Interactions, Lottie, or dotLottie — giving you smoother performance and lower file sizes.

### **Will our team be able to update the site easily?**

Yes.  
 Your new system will be component-based, modular, and documented.  
 Teams can update content, publish new pages, and duplicate patterns without developers.

### **What happens to hosting after the migration?**

Everything moves to Webflow’s native hosting — fast, secure, CDN-backed, and maintenance-free.

### **Do you support us after launch?**

Yes.  
 Two weeks of free post-launch support are included.

### **Do you handle urgent migrations?**

If the timeline is realistic and decisions move quickly, yes.  
 We can enable a structured “rush mode.”

### **Do you migrate Framer, Wix, Squarespace, or custom sites?**

Yes — WordPress is just the most common use case.
