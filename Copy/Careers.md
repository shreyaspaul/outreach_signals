## How we work

- A small core team. Never more than three projects at a time.
- Remote-first. Async-friendly. We respect time zones.
- Founder-led. No agency layers, no account managers.
- Senior involvement on every project, every day.
- Quality bar over speed bar — when we have to choose.

---

## Who fits here

Designers, Webflow developers, motion artists, and writers who:

- Have a strong portfolio and genuinely care about craft
- Communicate clearly in writing
- Take ownership without being managed
- Want to do the best work of their career, not the most

---

## How we hire

We don't run constant open roles. We keep a small network of specialists we trust, and bring people in on projects that match their strengths.

If that sounds like a fit, send us your work. We'll keep it on file and reach out when something lines up.
