## **SEO: Visibility and rankings (Google)**

## SEO is about showing up when people are actively looking for a solution like yours.

## This is where the work actually happens:

* ## **Keyword and intent research**    We identify what your buyers are searching for, how competitive those queries are, and which ones your site should realistically win. 

* ## **Page-level optimisation**    Titles, meta descriptions, headings, copy structure, and on-page clarity, aligned to both keywords and search intent. 

* ## **Site structure and internal linking**   Pages are organised so search engines understand what matters most, and authority flows to priority pages. 

* ## **Technical SEO foundation**    Crawling, indexing, redirects, URL structure, sitemaps, robots rules, and clean markup. 

* ## **Performance and Core Web Vitals**    Page speed, script load, image optimisation, and accessibility handled. 

* ## **Backlink building through trusted networks**   We help you build high-quality backlinks through established networks and placements relevant to your category, which helps with authority building.     

* ## **Search analytics and funnel tracking**    GA4, Search Console, and event tracking set up so you can see what traffic converts.

## This work determines whether your site **ranks, attracts qualified traffic, and sustains visibility over time**.

## **AEO: Interpretation and citation (AI systems)**

## AEO is about how your product is *understood* once it’s discovered — especially by AI assistants.

## This requires additional, deliberate work beyond SEO:

* ## **Query and answer mapping**    We identify the questions AI tools should answer using your product — definitions, use cases, comparisons, and recommendations. 

* ## **AI-readable content structures**    Pages are shaped so LLMs can clearly extract answers, summaries, and explanations without guessing. 

* ## **Answer blocks designed for citation**    We create structured sections that AI systems can quote, summarise, or reference directly. 

* ## **Schema for AI understanding**    Product, pricing, feature, FAQ, article, and org schema applied in ways that help AI interpret what your product is and where it fits. 

* ## **Hierarchy and context refinement**    Page relationships are clarified so AI understands how features, use cases, and concepts connect. 

* ## **LLM testing and iteration**    We actively test how ChatGPT, Gemini, Claude, and Perplexity describe and compare your product — then refine until the outputs are accurate.

## This work determines whether AI assistants **explain your product correctly, recommend it confidently, or ignore it entirely**.
