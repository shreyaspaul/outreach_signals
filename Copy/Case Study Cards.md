1. **Breadcrumb** — Category / Company Name
2. **Heading** — Narrative title with one impact metric (highlight this in orange/accent color)
3. **Subheading** — 2–3 sentences: problem → approach → result
4. **CTA** — "View case study"

## Notes for Designers

- The **bolded metric** in each heading is the piece styled in orange/accent color inline — same treatment as seen in the Qmin card reference image.
- Card layout: text content on the left, custom image on the right.
- Images are handled separately — one custom image per card.
- Qmin: No hard revenue number appears in the case study copy. If the client has confirmed a revenue figure separately, that can replace "Under 60 Seconds." The current metric is taken directly from the case study narrative.
