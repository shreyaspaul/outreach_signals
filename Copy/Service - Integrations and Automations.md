## **How Prismport approaches integrations & automations**

### **Built on the Webflow app ecosystem**

Webflow’s app ecosystem is broad, stable, and well-maintained. We use it intentionally — selecting apps that are actively supported, performant, and reliable. This keeps integrations clean and reduces long-term breakage or maintenance issues.

---

### **Experience across CRMs and business tools**

We work with a wide range of CRMs, analytics tools, marketing platforms, and internal systems. This includes both common tools and niche, industry-specific software used in areas like insurance, legal, and operations-heavy businesses.

---

### **Automation with Make and n8n**

For more complex workflows, we build automations using platforms like Make and n8n. This allows us to connect multiple systems, handle conditional logic, and manage data flow across tools without relying on fragile, single-purpose setups.

---

### **Thorough testing across real scenarios**

Integrations are tested across expected use cases and edge cases. We validate data handling, failure states, retries, and fallbacks so workflows behave reliably under real traffic and real user behavior.

---

### **Built for visibility and debugging**

We structure integrations so issues are easy to trace and diagnose. Clear naming, logging, and separation of concerns make it easier to understand what’s happening if something needs adjustment later.

---

### **Designed to fit existing operations**

Before building automations, we understand how your team already works — who uses the data, where it goes, and what actions follow. Integrations are designed to support existing processes, not force new ones unnecessarily.

## **How we build reliable integrations**

**1\. Map the workflow**  
 We diagram the full data flow in FigJam triggers, fields, dependencies, and real scenarios.

**2\. Fix the source of truth**  
 We clean your CRM or database before wiring anything. Consistent fields → fewer failures.

**3\. Build stable automations**  
 Using Make or n8n, we create workflows with validation, fallback logic, and duplicate prevention.

**4\. Test real scenarios**  
 We simulate failures, high-traffic sequences, and multi-step flows to ensure reliability.

**5\. Document everything**  
 Clear instructions on what connects to what, how to edit it, and how to extend it later.

## **FAQs**

**How do you make sure integrations don’t break during high-traffic launches?**  
 We build workflows with validation checks, fallback logic, and retries. We also pick plans that match your expected traffic so tasks don’t get throttled.

**Can our team update the automations later?**  
 Yes — with basic technical familiarity. Everything is documented clearly, and the workflows are structured so you can edit fields, steps, and routes safely. If you want us to maintain it, we can.

**What if our CRM setup is messy?**  
 We clean it before building anything. Fixing fields, duplicates, and naming is essential for stable sync. It’s usually the first thing we fix.

**Which platform is better — Make, n8n, or Zapier?**  
 Make and n8n for serious workflows. Zapier only for simple one-step tasks.  
 We pick the tool based on the workflow, not the other way around.

**Can Webflow support multi-step onboarding flows?**  
 Yes. With Webflow \+ Memberstack \+ Make/n8n, we can automate onboarding, access management, email sequences, gated content, invites, and more.

**Can you fix our existing broken workflows?**  
 If they’re salvageable, yes. If they’re too patchy or fragile, a clean rebuild is faster and far more reliable.

**Is this included in a Webflow revamp?**  
 Yes. All core CRM, analytics, and automation setups required for your new site are part of the redesign scope.
