## **What we help fix**

### **People don’t understand what you do**

When the website doesn’t clearly explain the product, the business pays for it elsewhere. Sales conversations start too early. Marketing has to over-explain. Inbound quality drops. We help teams clarify positioning, messaging, and page structure so the website does the explaining upfront, before a call, demo, or follow-up.

---

### **The site looks good, but doesn’t sell.**

Visual polish alone doesn’t create trust. Users look for clarity, consistency, and signals that the product is real and reliable. When those are missing, hesitation shows up as lower conversion and longer decision cycles. We design websites that feel deliberate and credible, not just visually refined.

---

### **The website becomes a bottleneck for the team**

If every update requires a developer, coordination, or fear of breaking something, the website slows the company down. Marketing ships less. Experiments get postponed. Teams stop touching the site. We build Webflow systems that teams can safely update and evolve without friction.

---

### **Performance issues quietly hurt results**

Slow pages and fragile interactions don’t always show up clearly in dashboards, but users feel them immediately. Performance affects trust, engagement, and conversion. We treat speed, stability, and clean execution as baseline requirements, not optimizations added later.

---

### **Your current platform is limiting growth**

For many teams, the problem isn’t a failed migration — it’s that their current platform no longer fits how the company operates. WordPress setups become fragile, plugin-heavy, and expensive to maintain. Updates feel risky. Teams start avoiding change. We help teams move to Webflow with structure, performance, and long-term maintainability in mind — not just a visual rewrite.

---

### **The site doesn’t scale as the company grows**

As products mature, websites often become patched collections of pages instead of coherent systems. Inconsistencies grow. Confidence drops. Changes take longer than they should. We design websites as systems that can grow alongside the product, not one-off builds that need replacing every year.

# **⭐ The Prismport Process**

*(Clear. Fast. Zero chaos.)*

**Heading**  
 A simple tested process that just works.

---

### **1\. Understand the business**

We start by going deep into how your company actually works — your product, your revenue model, your customer types, your positioning, your pains, and what your website needs to unlock for the business.  
 We study the current funnel, what customers struggle with, what they love, and where the messaging breaks.  
 This step gives us the context most agencies skip, helping us uncover opportunities, shape the narrative correctly, and design a website that feels built for your market. 

---

### **2\. Kickoff & Direction**

We map the full site: pages, structure, sitemap, and the core content each page needs.  
You’ll share references for what feels right, what doesn’t, and the visual direction that matches your vision. By the end of this step, page content is locked, the IA is clear, and everyone knows exactly what we’re designing.

### **3\. Design**

Design starts with wireframes shaped by the content and structure we agreed on.  
 We move into high-fidelity layouts in Figma, sections, components, interactions, and motion concepts, all shared in quick loops so feedback is easy.  
 We follow the reconfirm process so nothing is created in isolation; you’re always seeing direction early and part of the design process. 

---

### **4\. Webflow Development**

We translate the Figma system into a clean Webflow build using a scalable component architecture.  
CMS, integrations, accessibility, performance, and QA follow next.

---

### **5\. Launch & Handover**

You get a smooth launch, a stable build, and full visibility into how everything works.  
 We include documentation, Loom walkthroughs, and a modular system your team can update confidently without relying on us for routine edits.  
 Support stays open, and your site is ready to scale.

# **FAQs (trimmed, founder-centric)**

## **How do you work with founders during the project?**

We keep communication tight and transparent.  
You’ll be added to our Slack, where most of the work happens — async updates, quick clarifications, Loom videos, and design previews.

We share updates almost every day or every alternate day.  
 You’ll get live Figma links, read-only Webflow links, and a weekly call if you prefer.  
 You always know what’s happening and what’s coming next.

---

## **What happens if we don’t like the initial designs?**

We almost never reach that point — because the direction is aligned *before* design begins.

Here’s how we avoid misalignment:

* We study your product and customers directly

* We collect references for what you do and don’t like

* We build detailed moodboards you approve

* Design only starts when you’re aligned on the direction

By the time you see the first designs, they already reflect your taste, your product, and your brand.  
 This keeps the process smooth and fast.

---

## **Do you offer support after launch?**

Yes.  
 You get **two weeks of free post-launch support** to fix bugs, answer questions, refine small details, and help your team adjust to the new system.

If you want continued help, we offer ongoing support at **$35/hr**, simple and flexible.

---

## **How flexible are you with scope changes?**

Small changes are totally fine — and expected in founder-led projects.  
 Bigger scope shifts (new pages, new workflows, large redesigns) require a new estimate, so timelines remain realistic and the project stays healthy.

We’re flexible, but we don’t let scope creep silently balloon into chaos.  
 Everything stays clear and mutually agreed.

---

## **Do you help with copy and positioning?**

Yes — but we do this *with* you, not for you in isolation.

You know your product better than anyone.  
 We take your core content, refine the messaging, shape the narrative, and give it a tone and structure that makes sense for SaaS and tech buyers.

We help with:

* Tone of voice

* Homepage narrative

* Page structure & messaging hierarchy

* Differentiation

* Content clarity

Think of it as **strategic editing \+ narrative framing**, not ghostwriting your entire site without context.

---

## **Do you help with brand identity or visual direction?**

Yes.  
 We work with a curated group of specialists — brand designers, 3D artists, illustrators — who join your project when needed.

You meet them, review concepts with them, and get a brand identity that feels intentional, consistent, and modern.  
 It’s fully transparent and collaborative.

---

## **What if we need to move extremely fast?**

It’s possible — with conditions.  
 Fast timelines require fast decisions, clear inputs, and availability from your side.

If you need an accelerated timeline, we can discuss a **rush mode** with a rush fee.  
 But we never overpromise on speed.  
 We only commit when the timeline is realistic and our quality bar stays intact.

---

## **Can you fix our existing Webflow site?**

Yes — if the foundations are healthy.

If your site follows Webflow basics (clean CMS, logical class structure, frameworks not entirely broken), we can fix or extend it.

If the site is structurally messy — endless divs, unnamed classes, conflicting animations — a rebuild is usually *cheaper, cleaner, and faster* than trying to repair it.

We’ll assess and give you the best option.
