## Webflow Design FAQs

**What does your Webflow design service include?**
It typically includes information architecture, desktop and mobile UI design in Figma, a scalable component system, motion and interaction concepts, and visual assets like product UI mockups, illustrations, or diagrams where needed. Everything is designed to map directly to how Webflow is built — so there's no gap between what's designed and what gets shipped.

---

**Do I need an existing brand identity before you start?**
No. If your brand isn't fully defined, we establish a practical visual direction early in the process — typography, color, and usage rules that give the site consistency. For teams that need deeper brand work (logo, full identity system), we bring in specialist partners who work as part of the same project, not a separate engagement.

---

**Can you work from our existing Figma files?**
Yes. If you have a design system or partially built Figma files, we can extend them, clean them up, or use them as a starting point. We'll be honest if the existing structure makes more sense to rebuild than patch.

---

**Do you write copy, or is that on us?**
We work with your copy rather than for you in isolation. You provide the core content and product knowledge — we refine the messaging, sharpen the narrative, and structure it for clarity and conversion. Think of it as strategic editing rather than ghostwriting. If copy needs to be written from scratch, we can scope that separately.

---

**How involved do we need to be during the design process?**
Involved, but not burdened. We share work frequently and follow a reconfirmation process where direction is locked before we move deeper. You'll review moodboards, approve wireframes, and see the first UI direction early — so feedback happens at the right moments rather than all at the end.

---

**What if we don't like the initial design direction?**
We rarely reach that point because direction is aligned before design begins. We spend time on moodboards and references before touching layouts, so by the time you see the first designs, they already reflect your taste and product. If something isn't right, we course-correct quickly — before full designs are built out.

---

**Do you design mobile versions as well?**
Yes. Every page is designed for desktop and mobile as a baseline. Breakpoint behavior is considered during the design phase, not patched in later during development. Responsive behavior is part of the design system, not an afterthought.

---

**Will the final Webflow build match the design?**
Yes. We design specifically for how Webflow builds, so what you approve in Figma is what ships — spacing, typography, responsive behavior, and motion included. There's no "design versus reality" gap where the live site quietly drifts from the mockups. When something genuinely needs to adapt for a real browser, we make that call deliberately and show you, rather than letting the build diverge on its own.

---

**How long does the design phase typically take?**
Two to four weeks for most projects, depending on page count and the depth of visual work needed. Projects with complex animations, custom illustrations, or a brand identity component take longer. We'll give you a specific timeline during scoping.

## Webflow Development FAQs

**What makes your Webflow builds different from standard builds?**
Many Webflow sites look fine at launch but become fragile as teams try to scale them — unnamed classes, inconsistent components, and structures that break when you add pages. We build as systems from day one: reusable components, semantic markup, clean CMS architecture, and documented patterns. The result is a site your team can confidently extend without creating technical debt.

---

**What Webflow framework do you use, and why?**
Our default is Lumos. It scales cleanly across large sites, complex CMS setups, and growing teams, which keeps a site maintainable long after launch. We're also fluent in Client-First and can work within other conventions if your existing site requires it.

---

**How do you approach performance and Lighthouse scores?**
Performance is part of the craft for us, not a box we tick after launch. We optimize images, scripts, fonts, and animations as we build, and we test throughout rather than at the end. Most of our builds land strong scores across performance, accessibility, best practices, and SEO — often in the 90s — but the real goal is a site that feels genuinely fast for your visitors, not just a high number in a report.

---

**What types of animations can you build?**
We implement GSAP for complex scroll-based and timeline interactions, Lottie and dotLottie for lightweight product visuals and looping animations, Rive for state-based and interactive sequences, and Webflow-native interactions for transitions and reveals. Every animation goes through performance checks before launch — visual richness shouldn't come at the cost of load time.

---

**Can you build custom functionality beyond standard Webflow?**
Yes. When the product requires it, we write clean custom JavaScript, handle API integrations, build gated flows, set up webhooks, and manage logic that Webflow doesn't handle natively. Webflow is the foundation — it's not the ceiling.

---

**How do you handle CMS for content-heavy sites?**
We structure CMS collections around how you actually publish, not generic templates. That means collections reference each other correctly, fields are named and typed properly, and the content model scales as you add more. We also write custom code where needed to support dynamic filtering, conditional rendering, or non-standard display logic.

---

**Can you take over or fix an existing Webflow site?**
Yes, if the foundations are sound. If the site uses a clean structure with consistently named classes and logical component patterns, we can extend or repair it. If the build is structurally messy — conflicting interactions, unnamed classes, nested overrides — rebuilding is usually faster and cheaper. We'll assess and give you a straight answer before committing.

---

**Will our team be able to edit the site after you build it?**
Yes. The build is modular by design. Your team can update content, duplicate sections, add new pages, and publish without writing code. We provide Loom walkthroughs and structured documentation so updates feel straightforward, not stressful.

---

**How long does a Webflow build take?**
A typical build runs two to five weeks once designs are final, depending on page count, animation complexity, CMS depth, and integrations. Simpler sites move faster; heavily animated or content-rich builds take longer. We give you a specific timeline at scoping and keep you updated as we go, so the date you're planning around doesn't move without warning.

## Migrations & Rebuilds FAQs

**Will we lose our Google rankings when we migrate?**
Not if the migration is handled correctly. We transfer all metadata, URL slugs, redirects, schema, alt tags, internal linking, and indexing rules as part of the process. A short re-crawl period after launch is normal for any migration — but because Webflow loads faster and removes bloat, most teams see equal or better performance within a few weeks.

---

**What platforms can you migrate from?**
WordPress is the most common, but we also migrate from Wix, Squarespace, Framer, Webflow (restructure/rebuild), and custom-built sites on React or Next.js. The process is the same regardless of the source: audit, content mapping, SEO continuity, build, QA, launch.

---

**Do you migrate all our existing content and blog posts?**
Yes — posts, categories, tags, authors, publish dates, featured images, metadata, and internal links. We also help identify and remove content that's outdated, low-quality, or hurting rather than helping your rankings.

---

**Can we redesign the site during the migration?**
Yes, and we'd usually recommend it. Most WordPress designs are theme-constrained and feel dated by the time teams decide to migrate. Combining a migration with a visual refresh means you move to Webflow with a site that actually reflects your current product — not a pixel-for-pixel copy of what you're leaving.

---

**What if our current site is a structural mess?**
We assess first. If the content and SEO signals are salvageable, we migrate what matters and leave behind what doesn't. If the build is so fragile that working with it adds more risk than a clean start, we tell you directly. Rebuilding from scratch is sometimes the cheaper and faster path.

---

**How long does a migration take?**
Most migrations run three to six weeks, depending on the volume of content, complexity of the existing CMS, and whether a visual redesign is included. We'll map the scope in our first assessment and give you a specific timeline before work begins.

---

**Do you handle the DNS cutover and launch?**
Yes. We manage the full launch sequence — DNS changes, SSL provisioning, final QA on the live domain, and monitoring in the hours after cutover. We aim for zero downtime and stay available through the launch window to handle anything that surfaces.

---

**Is Webflow's hosting reliable enough for real traffic?**
Yes. Webflow hosting runs on AWS with a global CDN, automatic SSL, and no plugin infrastructure to maintain. There are no server configurations, no update cycles to manage, and no plugin conflicts. For most SaaS and startup marketing sites, Webflow's hosting is faster and more stable than what teams were running before.

## Integrations & Automations FAQs

**What tools can you integrate with Webflow?**
We regularly work with HubSpot, Pipedrive, Close, and Brevo for CRM; Airtable, Google Sheets, and Notion for databases; Memberstack for membership and access control; Make and n8n for automation workflows; and GA4, Segment, Mixpanel, and PostHog for analytics and event tracking. We also build against REST APIs, webhooks, and custom endpoints when the stack requires it.

---

**Can you integrate tools that aren't on your standard list?**
Usually yes. If the tool has a REST API, webhooks, or is supported by Make or n8n, we can connect it. We map the full integration during the assessment phase — if something isn't feasible, we tell you before starting.

---

**What's the difference between Make, n8n, and Zapier — and which should we use?**
Make and n8n are built for real workflows: multi-step logic, conditional branching, error handling, and volume. Zapier works for simple, single-step connections but becomes expensive and fragile when workflows get complex. We use Make or n8n for anything serious and only recommend Zapier for genuinely simple, low-volume tasks where the simplicity is worth it.

---

**How do you make sure automations don't lose data or create duplicates?**
We build deduplication checks, validation rules, and fallback logic into every workflow from the start. Before launch, we test against edge cases — form submissions under load, partial data, retry scenarios, and failure states. The goal is a workflow that handles real traffic reliably, not just happy-path demos.

---

**Can you fix automations or integrations someone else built?**
Yes, if they're salvageable. We audit the existing setup first and give you an honest assessment: fix it, refactor it, or rebuild it cleanly. Fragile workflows built on top of more fragile workflows usually cost more to maintain than a clean rebuild.

---

**Will our team be able to manage the automations after you build them?**
Yes, with basic technical familiarity. Every workflow is documented with clear diagrams showing what triggers what, where data goes, and how to modify steps safely. For non-technical teams, we can scope an ongoing maintenance arrangement if preferred.

---

**Is integration work included in a website project, or billed separately?**
Core integrations required for the site to function — CRM, analytics, form routing — are included in the redesign scope. More complex automation builds (multi-system workflows, Memberstack setups, custom data sync) are scoped separately with a clear estimate upfront.

## SEO & AEO FAQs

**What's the difference between SEO and AEO?**
SEO (Search Engine Optimisation) is about ranking in Google when people search for solutions like yours. AEO (Answer Engine Optimisation) is about how AI systems — ChatGPT, Gemini, Perplexity, Claude — understand, describe, and recommend your product. SEO helps users find your site. AEO determines what AI says about you when someone asks it a question your product should answer. Both matter, and they're built on the same underlying foundation of clear structure and content.

---

**Do I need both SEO and AEO, or is one enough?**
You need both — and increasingly, AEO is what separates companies that get recommended by AI from those that get ignored. Strong SEO alone doesn't guarantee that AI tools will describe your product accurately or cite it in relevant answers. AEO is the additional layer that ensures AI systems can extract, summarise, and reference your content correctly.

---

**What does "technically SEO-ready" actually mean in a Webflow build?**
It means: correct heading hierarchy (H1 → H2 → H3), descriptive and unique page titles and meta descriptions, properly attributed images with alt text, clean URL slugs, working canonical tags, a sitemap submitted to Search Console, correct robots.txt rules, schema markup applied where relevant, fast load times, no duplicate content issues, and internal links that flow authority to the pages that matter. These aren't optional extras — they're the baseline every build we ship meets.

---

**Is SEO included in a standard Webflow redesign?**
The technical SEO foundations — clean markup, semantic structure, metadata, schema, redirects, and indexing rules — are included in every Webflow build we ship. Deeper SEO work (keyword strategy, content architecture, backlink building, AEO implementation) is a separate engagement scoped based on your goals and current visibility.

---

**Can you do SEO work on a site we already have?**
Yes. We frequently work on live sites without a full redesign — auditing structure, fixing technical issues, improving on-page content, and implementing AEO layers. A full rebuild helps, but it's not a prerequisite for meaningful SEO improvements.

---

**How do you check whether AI tools are describing our product accurately?**
We test directly across ChatGPT, Gemini, Perplexity, and Claude using queries your buyers are likely to ask — category questions, comparison questions, use case questions, and direct product questions. We document what each system currently says about your product, where it's wrong or incomplete, and then implement the structural and content changes needed to improve those outputs. After implementation, we retest and iterate.

---

**Do you do link building?**
Yes. We help build high-quality backlinks through established networks and relevant category placements. We focus on placements that are editorially appropriate and genuinely useful, not volume-based schemes that create short-term noise and long-term risk.

---

**Can you guarantee first-page Google rankings?**
No — and it's worth being cautious of anyone who does. Rankings depend on competition, domain authority, and Google's evolving algorithm, none of which any agency fully controls. What we can do is build the technical foundation, content structure, and authority signals that give you the best possible chance, then track progress transparently so you can see what's moving and why.

---

**How long before we see results from SEO work?**
Technical SEO improvements and on-page changes typically show movement within four to twelve weeks, depending on how frequently Google crawls your site. Content and backlink work compounds over three to six months. AEO results can appear faster — AI tools update their understanding more frequently than search rankings shift. We track progress at every stage so you're not left guessing.
