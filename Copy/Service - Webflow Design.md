**Our Webflow design approach**

---

### **Conversion-focused layout design**

We design layouts around conversion: what users notice first, what they understand quickly, and what drives the next step. Hierarchy, spacing, and content emphasis are built to reduce hesitation and increase action.

---

### **Visuals that explain fast**

We design high-quality visuals, product UI, backgrounds, diagrams, and lightweight motion, that explain features and workflows quickly and hold attention while staying clean and modern.

---

### **Scalable Design System**

Everything is designed as a reusable system. Sections, components, and variants,mapped the way it will be built in Webflow. That gives your team a foundation that stays consistent as new pages get added.

---

### **You’re involved throughout**

We share work continuously and follow a reconfirmation process.You’re never seeing the design for the first time at the end, hence decisions stay aligned as the work progresses.

---

### **Small, senior design team**

Your project is handled by one or two senior designers, end-to-end. Direct collaboration keeps decisions fast and avoids the information loss that happens in large team handoffs.

### **What we design**

### **Branding**

We create practical, Web-ready brand foundations — the logo, typography, color direction, and usage rules that keep your site consistent. Simple, clear, and easy for your team to apply.

### **High-Fidelity Website Design**

Each page is designed in Figma with a clear layout system for desktop and mobile. You get polished sections, strong hierarchy, and the core components your site will reuse across future pages.

### **Motion & Interaction Design**

When motion adds clarity, we storyboard and design lightweight animations — scroll cues, micro-interactions, or small explainers — built to feel intentional and stay fast.

### **Illustrations & Visual Systems**

We create custom visuals, icons, and diagrams that match your brand and make explanations easier. Custom assets built for your product. 

**Figma Design System**

We prepare a clean component system in Figma that maps directly to Webflow, making the build predictable and giving you a scalable foundation for new pages later.

## **How We Design**

We keep the process tight, collaborative, and extremely transparent.

### **1\. Discovery & Direction**

We explore references, moodboards, and brand cues to establish a visual direction early

**2\. IA and Sitemap design**

 We map page flows based on the locked in content, set hierarchy, and set up layouts that fit the copy. 

**3\. High-Fidelity UI Design**

Once the structure is approved, we move into polished UI, layouts, visuals, messaging placement, and early motion thinking.

### **5\. Design System Assembly**

Components built exactly like how it would in Webflow. You’ll know exactly how to manage the Webflow components, beforehand.

## **FAQs**

**How early do we see visual directions?**  
 Usually in week one. We move fast so you get clarity early, not after a long research cycle.

**What if we don’t like the first direction?**  
 Unlikely — because we align heavily during moodboarding.  
 If needed, we adjust quickly before any full designs are created.

**Do you work with our internal designer?**  
 Yes. We can co-design, extend existing systems, or help formalize what you already have.

**How many pages are included?**  
 Depends on scope. Most projects include 5–12 core pages plus variants. We’ll scope this clearly before starting.

**What happens if our brand is not well defined?**  
 We can refine your existing identity or bring in one of our specialist partners for deeper brand work — still under one unified process.

**Do you design for complex SaaS products?**  
 Yes. Much of our work involves simplifying sophisticated workflows into clear, trust-building visuals.

**How long does design typically take?**  
 2–4 weeks for most projects, depending on page count and complexity.
