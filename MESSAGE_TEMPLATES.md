## A/B TESTING SCHEDULE

**Week 1-2**: Test connection request length
- Group A: Short (under 200 chars)
- Group B: Longer (250-300 chars)

**Week 3-4**: Test first message data vs. story
- Group A: Heavy metrics and numbers
- Group B: Case study narrative

**Week 5-6**: Test CTA type
- Group A: "Worth 15 min?"
- Group B: "Mind if I send the breakdown?"

**Week 7-8**: Test subject line (email)

Track accept rates, reply rates, and meeting book rates for each variant.

## FINAL NOTES

**These templates are starting points, not scripts.** The best messages:

1. Reference something SPECIFIC about their company (recent funding, press, hiring, product launch)
2. Use THEIR DATA (exact PageSpeed score, exact traffic, exact bounce rate)
3. Sound like YOU (not a template)
4. Focus on THEIR PROBLEM (not your solution)
5. Offer VALUE FIRST (audit, breakdown, diagnostic) before asking for time

The goal is to sound like a peer who noticed something about their business they should know about - not a vendor trying to sell them something.
