# LinkedIn Outreach Strategy - Executive Summary

## The Bottom Line

Your original signal strategy (Funded + WordPress) was too narrow - only **27 prospects** in 316 graded entries.

The real opportunity: **198 funded companies with measurable website quality issues**, regardless of CMS platform. The signal isn't WordPress vs. Webflow - it's the gap between their growth stage and their website's ability to support it.

## Message Framework

### Connection Request (300 char max)
```
[Specific observation] + [Why it matters] + [Low-friction close]
```

**Example**:
```
Saw you hit 300K visits/month. Your mobile PageSpeed is 36 - at that traffic, every second costs you conversions. Worth a quick look?
```

---

### First Message (After Accept)
```
[Data-backed insight] + [Business implication] + [Soft question]
```

**Example**:
```
Your site's getting 1M+ visits/month but loads in 8 seconds on mobile. At that volume, every second of delay costs you 7-10% of conversions.

We've rebuilt sites for funded companies at your stage - typically see 20-40% conversion lifts just from speed improvements.

No pitch, but if you want to see what's slowing you down, I can send a breakdown. Worth 15 min?
```

## What Makes These Work

### DO:
✓ Use their specific data (271K visits, mobile 50, bounce 79%)
✓ Lead with business impact, not technical features
✓ Reference funding/growth stage
✓ Sound like a peer, not a vendor
✓ Offer value before asking for time
✓ Keep LinkedIn <100 words

### DON'T:
✗ Trash their current platform
✗ Use buzzwords ("cutting-edge," "best-in-class")
✗ Send generic templates
✗ Ask for 60-min calls upfront
✗ Pitch features (Webflow's CMS, etc.)
✗ Sound desperate ("just checking in")

## Critical Insights from Data Analysis

### What We Learned:

1. **Strict signals are too narrow**
   - Signal 1 (Funded + WordPress): 27 companies
   - Signal 2 (50K+ traffic + mobile <50): 6 companies
   - Signal 3 (Premium analytics + WordPress): 1 company

2. **Expand the aperture**
   - High traffic + slow (ANY platform): 10 companies
   - Funded + quality issues: 198 companies
   - Platform doesn't matter - performance does

3. **Data quality is excellent**
   - 93.7% have PageSpeed data
   - 81% have traffic data
   - 100% have design/content scores
   - Can personalize every message with real numbers

4. **WordPress isn't the enemy**
   - Some WordPress sites perform well
   - Some Webflow sites perform poorly
   - Focus on outcomes, not platforms

5. **Multiple issues = stronger hook**
   - Companies with 2+ problems (design + performance, or content + speed) are more receptive
   - Easier to quantify total impact
   - More urgency

## Key Takeaway

**Stop focusing on "WordPress companies" and start focusing on "funded companies with measurable website gaps."**

The CMS is just implementation detail. The real signal is:
1. They have traction (traffic, funding)
2. Their website is provably underperforming (data you have)
3. That gap is costing them money (quantifiable)

Your job is to:
1. Show them the gap with THEIR data
2. Quantify what it's costing them
3. Offer to fix it

Do that 40 times/week and you'll book 1-2 meetings/week within 30 days.
